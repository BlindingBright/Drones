Bluejay3D Exclusive
8 (37.6% Limit / 9.4% Floor)

Replaces stock Bluejay's hardcoded Pwm_Limit_Beg (~2%) braking cap and prevents run2 from overwriting braking power. Scales active reverse-phase braking proportionally between floor and limit.

5 (64 / ~5,000 eRPM)

Speed threshold (Comm_Period4x_H) where active braking completes and commutation flips to the new direction. If rotor is already slower than this threshold on stick flip, reverses immediately in 0 braking steps (AM32 style).

3 (2.35% Duty Floor)

Prevents low-throttle 3D commands (DShot 48..100 and 1048..1100) near the 3D deadband from starving the BEMF comparator or getting mistaken for Flag_Rcp_Stop.

1 (2 Confirmations >20k eRPM)

Number of consecutive opposite-direction commutations required above 20,000 eRPM before engaging 180° braking. Prevents single DShot bit errors from causing mid-air violent braking.
