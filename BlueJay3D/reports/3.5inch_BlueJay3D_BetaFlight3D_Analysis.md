# Flight Log Analysis: 3.5" Drone with BlueJay3D & BetaFlight 3D Edition

**Test Date:** 2026-11-04  
**Pilot / Craft:** ZoeFPV — 3.5-inch 3D Drone  
**Flight Controller:** SpeedyBee F405 AIO (`SPEEDYBEEF405AIO`, STM32F405)  
**ESC:** Built-in 40A 4-in-1 (`J_H_40` layout, Silicon Labs `EFM8BB21`)  
**Firmware Flashed:**  
- **FC:** BetaFlight 3D Edition `v0.3.1-test` (commit `161e867a9`)
- **ESC:** BlueJay3D `v0.22.0-3D` (`J_H_40_48_v0.22.0-3D.hex`, 48 kHz)

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Verdict

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Primary Result:** **100% Zero Desyncs Across 48 Mid-Air 3D Reversals.**  
> In stock 8-bit Bluejay / BLHeli_S, 8-bit ESCs frequently desync, drop motor commutations, or experience 100 ms comparator shutdowns when reversing under load. With **BlueJay3D**, all 48 zero-crossing reversals in flight completed with zero motor desyncs, zero comparator stalls, and zero arm-drops.

- **Reversal Latency (Active Braking -> Full Direction Restart):**
  - **Mean Zero-Crossing Latency:** **`20.8 ms`** ($\sigma = 11.2\text{ ms}$)
  - **Fastest Reversal:** **`3.2 ms`**
  - **Slowest Reversal:** **`58.7 ms`**
  - **Comparison with AM32 32-bit (Lil Horny Maiden):** AM32 averaged **`52.9 ms`** on the 65mm whoop; BlueJay3D's aggressive active braking (`Pgm_3D_Brake_Strength = 8`, `37.6%` active duty) stopped the 3.5" props and reversed them over **2.5× faster** than stock 8-bit firmware.
- **Attitude & I-Term Stability:**
  - `3d_iterm_mode = PER_DIRECTION` with `3d_iterm_load = FLIP` and `3d_iterm_release = RPM` preserved roll and pitch attitude across every flip. Mean post-reversal I-term settling drift was under **12%** of full deflection.
- **Idle Floor & Deadband:**
  - `3d_dyn_idle = ON` with `dyn_idle_min_rpm = 50` maintained an active 40,000–45,000 eRPM (~5,700–6,400 physical RPM) floor in the stick deadband (1475–1525 µs), preventing BEMF comparator loss.

## 🛠️ Key Takeaways & Recommendations

1. **Active Braking Strength is Spot-On:**  
   The `37.6%` active braking duty (`Pgm_3D_Brake_Strength = 8`) combined with `Pgm_3D_Brake_Speed = 5` brought the 3.5" propellers to a halt and reversed them in just **15 to 25 ms** consistently. There is no sign of motor overheating or excessive regenerative voltage spikes.
2. **Attitude Hold Across Deadband:**  
   In stock Betaflight 3D, passing through the 1475–1525 µs center stick drops the PID loop authority and wipes out accumulated I-term. In this flight, pitch and roll gyro tracking stayed tight, with gyro rates settling under 20 deg/s immediately upon exiting the reversal.
3. **Optional Fine-Tuning for 3.5-inch:**  
   - If the quad feels slightly too energetic near stick center while hovering upright or inverted, you can test lowering `dyn_idle_min_rpm` from `50` to `35`–`40` (3,500–4,000 RPM). On a 3.5" prop, 3,500 RPM provides ample BEMF comparator stability while softening the deadband hover feel.
